Original UI close actions: implementation rationale and evidence limits

Scope
CoolapkDisplay's close strategy calls the official Skip/Close action after the
official lifecycle method. It does not change advertisement data, validity checks,
SDK callback methods, networking, identity, pagination, media or gestures.

This is an early UI close. It can cause the application's normal close callbacks
and cleanup to happen earlier. It would be inaccurate to claim that SDK behavior
or all callback timing is unchanged. The plugin does not fabricate impressions,
load success, render success or an SDK close event directly.

Splash
- Exact controller: _TtC10CoolMarket33SplashAdvertisementViewController.
- Metadata gives viewDidAppear: as v20@0:8B16 and didTapSkipButton as v16@0:8.
- didTapSkipButton at main-file offset 0x5300c4 enters the official hide(animated:)
  routine at 0x532cf0.
- Disassembly shows a hideCalled guard, timer invalidation/release, and main-queue
  dispatch to an existing application closure. Its thunk 0x5330fc enters 0x52fe34.
- The plugin uses the runtime-validated original method IMP, never these raw
  offsets or a guessed Swift object layout.

Interstitial
- Exact view: _TtC10CoolMarket20KAInterstitialAdView (UIView descendant).
- didTapClose has ABI v16@0:8; didMoveToWindow is inherited from the UIView chain.
- Official action 0x3bf768 enters 0x3bfa10 and creates its own 0.25-second UIKit
  animation/completion. The original completion thunk 0x3c0628 enters ObjC stub
  0x102b10020. Any official didCloseBlock remains part of the existing action path.
- The plugin does not manually remove the modal or invoke didCloseBlock. The
  application keeps responsibility for its close animation and cleanup.

Safety of dispatch
- Every lifecycle and action signature is checked before changing the class.
- Inherited methods are overridden on the target class; parents are not edited.
- Original lifecycle receives the original arguments before any queued action.
- Main-queue tasks hold receiver, surface and window weakly and recheck identity
  and attachment before acting. No timer polls for the view.
- Manual and queued action share an invocation guard. Interstitial detachment
  invalidates old tickets and resets the next attachment's state. The splash
  controller guard is once per controller instance.
- Controllers already dismissing, moving away, or presenting another controller
  are not automatically skipped at that moment.
- Entire BUSplashView/BUSplashCardView/interstitial containers are not hidden by
  the visibility installer, preserving their fallback controls and closing path.

What still requires a real-device check
Simulator fixtures test dispatch correctness, lifetime, queue cancellation and
isolation. They do not prove that all real SDK providers share the same lifecycle,
that every advertisement is covered, or that modified-client restrictions will
not occur. An unrecognized class or incompatible action is left untouched.
